APEX RUSH - WebGL 3D racing game (Three.js r128, MIT)

Run: open index.html in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari, mobile too).
If your browser blocks local files, serve the folder:  python3 -m http.server 8000  -> http://localhost:8000

Controls: Arrows/WASD drive, Space handbrake, Shift nitro. Touch: on-screen buttons (auto throttle).
Drift to charge nitro. 3 laps vs 3 AI cars.
